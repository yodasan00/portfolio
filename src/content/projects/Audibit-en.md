**Audibit** is an advanced Android music player featuring a custom real-time **8D spatial audio engine** and offline media downloader.

- Developed a custom real-time **8D spatial audio engine** by extending **AndroidX Media3's audio pipeline** with DSP-based audio processing.
- Integrated native **yt-dlp**, **aria2c**, and **FFMPEG** binaries directly into the Android runtime for fast offline music downloading and format conversion.
- Designed a retro-inspired pixel/neumorphic UI with dedicated home-screen widgets and background notification playback controls.
- Optimized C++ audio rendering loops for low-latency DSP processing on mobile devices.
