Developed for the **Department of Information Technology, Govt. of Sikkim**, this multi-departmental ticket management application streamlines IT support and resolution workflows across government offices.

- Architected the complete ticket lifecycle system from submission, automated assignment, escalation, to resolution.
- Built a modular frontend in **Angular** featuring real-time analytical dashboards, SLA trackers, and status notifications.
- Designed secure **Django REST APIs** with **PostgreSQL** relational schemas and granular **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)**.
- Reduced inter-departmental ticket resolution latency through automated workflow routing.
